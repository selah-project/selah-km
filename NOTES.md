  ("to the heavens") as ព្រះអម្ចាស់ ("the Lord") — the Khmer-Bible

  transliterations — strip the Hebrew, keep the Khmer.
- Khmer is **isolating**: no suffix-morphology mapping needed at the
  Name seats (unlike so/az).

  brackets is bleed; Cyrillic must be checked on the RAW line
